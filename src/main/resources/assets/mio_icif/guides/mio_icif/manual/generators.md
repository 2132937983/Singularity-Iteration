# Generators

Generators make EU from fuel, sunlight, wind, water, heat or kinetic energy.
Each page gives the output voltage tier and the output in EU/t.

## The two solar routes

Solar generators start from the basic solar generator, split into two routes and meet again at the quantum generator.

| Item | Concentrator route | Photon resonance route |
|---|---|---|
| Machines | Advanced → Hybrid → Ultimate hybrid → Quantum solar | Advanced (METS) → Photon resonance → Ultimate photon resonance |
| Rated output by day | 12 / 96 / 768 / 6,144 EU/t | 48 / 384 / 3,072 EU/t |
| At night | 0 | 15 / 25 / 35 % of the rating (clear night) |
| Full output from | 850 W/m² | 600 / 450 / 350 W/m² |
| Under cover | nothing | keeps 30 / 45 / 60 % of the sky light |
| Rain / thunderstorm | about 37 % / almost nothing | about 85 % / about 61 % |
| Temperature | sensitive; the hybrid tiers cool the cells | hardly matters |
| Best for | sunny places without rain, such as deserts | rainy places, under cover, power at night |

The quantum generator is made from the top of each route (the quantum solar panel and the ultimate photon resonance solar generator). It makes 8,192 EU/t at all times, in any weather.
