# Quantum Generator

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:generator/block_quantum_generator" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The machine makes EU without fuel or sunlight, day and night, in any weather and temperature.
The machine is where the two solar routes meet: it is made from a quantum solar panel (the top of the concentrator route) and an ultimate photon resonance solar generator (the top of the photon resonance route).
The machine sends EU to the blocks on its six faces.

## Background

This section is background lore. It does not change the values of the machine.

The quantum generator takes its energy from the zero-point fluctuations of the vacuum.
- The quantum cores hold a stable vacuum state in a cavity inside the machine. The electromagnetic field in a vacuum always fluctuates: pairs of virtual photons appear and annihilate again within a very short time.
- The field confinement structure of the matter fabricator pulls the virtual photon pairs apart before they annihilate, so they become real photons.
- The two solar panels no longer receive outside sunlight. They receive the photons made in the cavity: the photon resonance panel turns the photon energy directly into charge separation, and the concentrator panel focuses the remaining photons onto an absorber and turns that heat into power.
- The iridium casing shields the cavity, so no outside disturbance can break the vacuum state. This is why the output stays constant in any time of day, weather and temperature.

## Procedure

1. Place the generator next to a machine or a cable.
2. Open the GUI to read the output and the voltage tier.

## Notes

- The GUI shows the status only. The GUI has no controls.
