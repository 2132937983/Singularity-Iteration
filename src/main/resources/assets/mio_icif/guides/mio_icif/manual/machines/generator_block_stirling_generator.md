# Stirling Generator

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:generator/block_stirling_generator" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The machine converts heat (HU) into EU.
The machine takes heat only through its back face.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place a heat generator with its front face against the back face of the Stirling Generator.
2. Connect an MV cable to the Stirling Generator.
3. Supply the heat generator with fuel or EU.
