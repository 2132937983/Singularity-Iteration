# Radioisotope Heat Generator

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:hugenerator/block_rt_heat_generator" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The machine makes heat (HU) from Radioisotope Fuel Pellets.
Each added pellet doubles the heat output. The pellets never deplete.
The machine gives heat only through its front face.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the machine with its front face against the heat consumer.
2. Put up to six Radioisotope Fuel Pellets into the pellet slots.
