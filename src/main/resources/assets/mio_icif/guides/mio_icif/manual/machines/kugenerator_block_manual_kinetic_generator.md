# Manual Kinetic Generator

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:kugenerator/block_manual_kinetic_generator" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The machine makes kinetic energy (KU) when a player right-clicks it.
Each click sends KU to the adjacent KU consumers and uses player food points.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the machine next to a Kinetic Generator.
2. Right-click the machine with an empty hand.
3. Repeat the click to keep the KU flow.

## Notes

- The machine works only when your food level is at least six points (three drumsticks).
