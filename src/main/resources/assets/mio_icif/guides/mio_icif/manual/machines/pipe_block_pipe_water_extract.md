# Water Extraction Pipe

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:pipe/block_pipe_water_extract" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Water Extraction Pipe pulls fluid from the block at its back and pushes the fluid out of its front.
EU from an LV cable increases the transfer speed. Without EU, the pipe moves fluid at a low base speed.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Look at the source tank and place the pipe against the tank.
2. Connect Water Pipes to the front of the extraction pipe.
3. Connect an LV cable to the extraction pipe to increase the speed.

## Notes

- The extraction pipe connects only on its front and on its back.
