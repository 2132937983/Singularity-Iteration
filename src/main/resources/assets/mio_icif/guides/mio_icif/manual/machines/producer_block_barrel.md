# Barrel

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:producer/block_barrel" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Barrel brews beer from wheat, Hops and water. The Barrel brews rum from sugar cane and water.
The beer changes to a stronger stage as the brew time increases. The Barrel uses no EU.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Right-click the Barrel to open the GUI.
2. Put the ingredients and a Water Bucket into the slots.
3. Sneak and right-click the Barrel to see the brew progress.
4. Right-click the Barrel with a Stone Mug to collect the drink.
