# Advanced Titanium Alloy Blast Furnace

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:producer/block_blast_furnace_advanced" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Advanced Titanium Alloy Blast Furnace uses heat (HU) and Compressed Air to process the Blast Furnace recipes.
The furnace works faster than the Blast Furnace. The furnace takes heat only from its front face.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place a heat generator against the front face of the furnace.
2. Put Compressed Air Cells into the air slot.
3. Put the input item into the input slot and wait until the furnace is hot.
4. Take the products and the Empty Cells from the output slots.

## Notes

- A redstone signal keeps the furnace hot and pauses the process.
