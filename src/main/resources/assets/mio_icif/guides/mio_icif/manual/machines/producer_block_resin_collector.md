# Resin Collector

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:producer/block_resin_collector" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Resin Collector collects Sticky Resin from a rubber log behind the collector. The block uses no EU.
The collector takes the resin when the resin spot on the log refills. The collector has one slot.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Find a rubber log with a resin spot.
2. Place the Resin Collector on the face of the log with the resin spot.
3. Open the collector and take the Sticky Resin.

## Notes

- When the slot is full, the collector leaves the resin on the log.
