# Solar Distiller

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:producer/block_solar_distiller" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Solar Distiller turns water into Distilled Water with sunlight. The machine uses no EU.
The machine works only in the Overworld, in daytime, in clear weather and with open sky above.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the Solar Distiller under open sky in the Overworld.
2. Put Water Buckets or Water Cells into the water input slot, or connect a water pipe.
3. Put Empty Cells or buckets into the distilled water slot to collect the product.
