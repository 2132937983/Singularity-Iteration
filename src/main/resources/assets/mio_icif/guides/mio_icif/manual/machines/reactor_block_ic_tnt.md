# Industrial TNT

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:reactor/block_ic_tnt" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

Industrial TNT is an explosive block with a stronger blast than vanilla TNT.
A redstone signal, fire, a burning projectile or a nearby explosion ignites the block. Industrial TNT is also the main charge of the Nuclear Bomb.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the Industrial TNT at a safe distance from your base.
2. Place a lever next to the TNT.
3. Turn on the lever.
4. Move away from the TNT before the fuse ends.

## Notes


> **WARNING:** A right-click on the block with any item in your hand ignites the TNT.
