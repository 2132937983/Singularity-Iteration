# Nuclear Bomb

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:reactor/block_reactor_nuke" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Nuclear Bomb has eight slots for Industrial TNT and one slot for nuclear material. The charge sets the explosion power.
A redstone signal arms the bomb and starts a countdown. The explosion leaves a radiation zone.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the bomb far away from your base and from other players.
2. Right-click the bomb to open the GUI.
3. Put Industrial TNT and nuclear material into the slots.
4. Read the explosion power in the GUI.
5. Apply a redstone signal to the bomb.
6. Move far away before the countdown ends.

## Notes


> **WARNING:** You cannot stop the countdown after you arm the bomb.


> **WARNING:** The explosion destroys a large area. The radiation zone stays after the explosion and harms living creatures.
