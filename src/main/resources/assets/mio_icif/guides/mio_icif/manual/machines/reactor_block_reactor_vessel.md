# Reactor Pressure Vessel

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:reactor/block_reactor_vessel" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The Reactor Pressure Vessel is a structural part of the fluid reactor. The vessel has no GUI and no function of its own.
The vessel forms the outer shell of the 5x5x5 fluid reactor. The vessel has a high blast resistance.

## Power data

This block uses no EU.

## Procedure

1. Place the Nuclear Reactor Generator at the center of a 5x5x5 area.
2. Put a Reactor Chamber on each of the six faces of the reactor.
3. Fill the outer layer of the 5x5x5 cube with pressure vessels.
4. Replace one or more vessels in the outer layer with ports or hatches.

## Notes

- The shell needs at least one Fluid Port, Access Hatch or Redstone Port.
