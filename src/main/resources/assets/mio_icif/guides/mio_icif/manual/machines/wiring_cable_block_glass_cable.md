# IV Cable

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="mio_icif:wiring/cable/block_glass_cable" scale="3" />
</Row>

## Function

The IV Cable carries EU at the IV voltage tier.
The cable does not shock living entities.
A packet above the cable limit burns the cable.

## Power data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated voltage tier | IV (8,192 EU) |

The game measured these values from the block entity of this version.

## Procedure

1. Connect the IV source to the IV machine with IV Cable.
2. Right-click the cable with a dye to give the cable a colour.
3. Give two parallel lines different colours to keep the lines apart.

## Notes

- Cables with different colours do not connect. A black cable connects to all colours.
- You can lay the cable through an iron scaffold and foam the scaffold. The cable conducts inside the reinforced stone.
