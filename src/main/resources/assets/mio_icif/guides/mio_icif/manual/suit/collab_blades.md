# Collab blades

With SlashBlade Resharped installed, Singularity Iteration adds three named collab blades: **Nano Blade → Quantum Blade → Singularity Blade**. They gather SlashBlade's proud souls and refines as usual, but their slash arts are paid with Singularity Iteration's resources. Without SlashBlade nothing on this page exists.

## Getting them

Each blade is made at a crafting table with SlashBlade's upgrade recipe, from materials Singularity Iteration already has:

| Blade | Type | Blade needed | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano Blade | uchigatana | any SlashBlade, refine 10, 2000 proud souls | <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_nanosaber" />, carbon plates, advanced circuits, an energy crystal |
| Quantum Blade | shallow tachi | the Nano Blade, refine 20, 5000 proud souls | <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_quantum_sword" />, a quantum core, iridium plates, super circuits |
| Singularity Blade | odachi | the Quantum Blade, refine 40, 10000 proud souls | molecular transformer cores, super iridium compressed plates, deuterium and tritium cells |

## Energy and readout

The blades hold EU: Nano 200k, Quantum 400k, Singularity 2M. They expose an FE item energy storage, so **the Armory charges them** while they are stored there (a fully repaired blade is no longer broken), and so do other FE chargers.

With a collab blade in the main hand, a card right of the hotbar shows its name, its EU and its own resource. A slash art the blade cannot pay does not fire; the action bar says why.

## The three blades

| Blade | Resource | Slash art | Special effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | EU and edge heat: an art costs 4000 EU and 35 heat; a hit 3 heat; at 100 it overheats until it has vented to 40 | **Piezo Cleave**: a standing wave along the cut, then three afterimage cuts almost at once | **Piezo Edge**: with EU, hits add armour-piercing vibration damage (2 to 6, hotter is more); from 70 heat they set the target on fire; carried, nanobots mend it with its own EU |
| Quantum | 30 coherence with a quantum core; without one the collapse is forced with 12000 EU | **Collapse Cut**: probability clouds on every target the suit locked or the blade entangled; the fringes close in and it collapses onto all of them at once | **Entanglement**: hits entangle the target for 10 seconds (at most 6), so the Collapse Cut finds them without locks |
| Singularity | Mass and containment: kills feed mass (bosses and players 5); holding mass costs 8 EU a tick, without EU the mass leaks; an art costs 40000 EU and 10 mass | **Event Horizon**: a micro-singularity where you look; the view bends round it into an Einstein ring, everything within 8 blocks is pulled in and torn, and it bursts in magenta Hawking radiation | **Tidal Shear**: hits add magic damage of mass ÷ 20 (at most 5) and draw the target to you |

The bending of the Event Horizon is a screen effect: it follows the warp screen effect option and is off while a shader pack is in use.

## Suit synergy

- **Nano Blade + nano suit**: the edge dumps its heat into the suit's fins and vents twice as fast.
- **Quantum Blade + quantum core**: the Collapse Cut is paid with coherence and spans up to 8 targets (6 without the core).
- **Singularity Blade + an electric suit**: when the blade is empty, the suit powers the containment and the mass does not leak.

## Wingmen

A Wingman holding a collab blade fires its slash art between combos, only when the blade can pay it (the quantum collapse with EU). Its blade cools and holds its containment as usual, its kills feed the singularity, and back in the Armory it is charged and repaired.
