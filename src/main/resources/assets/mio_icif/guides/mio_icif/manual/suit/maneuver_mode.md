# Special Maneuver Mode

## Function

Special Maneuver Mode is a flight mode of the quantum chestplate and the advanced quantum chestplate.
One switch starts all parts of the mode: free flight, the throttle, the drift, the flight pose, the vapor effects and the flight HUD.
The sprint key or the boost key opens the throttle. When you release the key, the throttle closes.
The suit flies along the view axis. Speed is throttle x 80 m/s.
At high speed the suit does not turn at once. It drifts wide through the turn and then follows the view axis.
At throttle 60 % (48 m/s) the suit breaks the shock barrier. You hear a boom and see a vapor cone and a shock ring.
Wake trails come from the hands and the feet at high speed.

## Procedure

1. Open the equipment console.
2. Select the chestplate.
3. Switch on Special Maneuver Mode.
4. Stand on a block. Press jump two times to launch.
5. Hold the sprint key or the boost key to open the throttle.
6. Look in the direction that you want to fly.
7. Tap sneak to lock the throttle. Tap sneak again to unlock it.
8. Release the sprint key to close the throttle and hover.
9. Land on a block to stop the flight.

## Hover

With the throttle closed, the suit hovers. The body leans forward a little.
Use the movement keys to drift slowly. Use jump and sneak to climb and descend.

## Flight HUD

The pitch ladder turns with the bank angle. The ring shows the flight path.
The left tape shows the speed in km/h. The right tape shows the altitude.
The bottom gauge shows the throttle. The line on the gauge is the shock barrier.

## Power data

The flight uses 6 EU/t plus 30 x speed x speed EU/t. Speed is in blocks per tick.
Slow cruise (22 m/s) uses about 42 EU/t. Full throttle uses about 486 EU/t.
A full quantum chestplate gives about 3 hours of slow cruise or 17 minutes at full throttle.

## Notes

The switch starts off. The jetpack keeps its normal controls while the switch is off.
If you hit a wall, the throttle closes.
Set the camera roll and the speed field of view in the mod configuration screen.
