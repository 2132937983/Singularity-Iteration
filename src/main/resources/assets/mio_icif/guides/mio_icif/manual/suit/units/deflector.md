# Quantum Deflector

<ItemImage id="mio_icif:module/item_module_deflector" scale="3" />

## Function

The unit cancels the damage that the armor lets through.
It spends 4000 EU from the chestplate for each damage point and stops at most 12 points per hit.
The field is not visible when idle. A hit lights hexagonal cells and deflector plates at the impact point.
The unit does not stop damage that bypasses armor, except explosions.

## Power data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Fits | Chestplate |
| Power | 4000 EU per damage point, max 12 points per hit |
| Display | No visor needed |

## Procedure

1. Install the unit into the quantum chestplate.
2. Charge the chestplate.
3. Check the chestplate charge after each fight.

## Notes

- The unit has no continuous drain. It uses EU only when it stops a hit.
