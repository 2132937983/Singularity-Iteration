# Tools

This chapter covers hand tools, weapons and carried devices. Each page gives the numbers, the keys and the rules.

## Common rules

- **Energy.** Electric tools store EU. A new tool is empty. The tooltip shows the tier, the cost per use and about how many uses are left.
- **Charging.** Energy storage blocks, charging pads, batteries, worn energy packs and the Armory charge tools. The tier is a label only; it does not limit charging. The tool's own charge rate limits the speed: tier 1 100, tier 2 128, tier 3 512, tier 4 2,048, tier 5 8,192 EU/t.
- **Energy packs.** A tool in hand takes energy from a worn energy pack before a use.
- **Without energy.** Drills and the chainsaw mine like a hand and drop nothing from blocks that need a tool. Weapons do not fire. A tool's attack bonus is gone.
- **G key.** Switches the mode of the tool in hand. The equipment console also selects modes. Mode messages show on the action bar.
- **Enchantments.** Every electric tool takes Energy Saving. By kind they also take: drills and the chainsaw (Efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch), the nanosaber and the quantum sword (sword enchantments), the nano bow (bow enchantments), the electric fishing rod (Lure, Luck of the Sea). Electric tools take no Unbreaking or Mending. Bronze tools are plain tools and take all their usual enchantments.
- **Claims.** Area tools and weapons check claim permission block by block. A protected block does not change.
