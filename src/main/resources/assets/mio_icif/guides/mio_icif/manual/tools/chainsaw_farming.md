# Chainsaw and Farm Tools

## Electric chainsaw

The <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_iron_chainsaw" /> is an electric axe and shears.

- Axe blocks: speed 8, 100 EU a block. Leaves, wool, cobwebs and vines: 25 EU a block.
- Right-click strips logs and scrapes copper, and shears sheep and other shearable mobs.
- **Felling mode (G).** Cutting one log fells the whole tree joined to it: diagonal branches too, up to 8 blocks sideways, at most 128 logs, each paid. Leaves stay.
- Attack 6 in hand; each hit costs energy, and without energy there is no damage.

## Electric hoe

The <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_iron_hoe" /> uses 10 EU per action per cell.

- G switches the tilling area: 1×1, 3×3, 5×5, 9×9.
- Seeds in the off hand are planted, fertilizer in the off hand is spread. Weeds go before tilling.
- **Chain harvest.** Right-click a ripe crop: the joined crops of the same kind (within 32 blocks of the click, at most 1,024) are harvested and replanted. Replanting takes a seed from the harvest. The drops land, merged, at the click. Fortune III is built in.

## Resin and others

- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_wooden_treetap" />: right-click a rubber tree's resin hole for 1–3 sticky resin; 16 uses.
- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_treetap_elc" />: 10 EU a use, 2–4 resin, +2 reach in hand.
- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:normal/item_weeding_trowel" />: right-click a crop stick to clear its weed (the weed drops). No durability.
- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_cutter" />: a crafting tool; sneak + right-click strips one insulation layer off a cable.
- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_hammer" />: a crafting tool; each craft costs one use.
