# Carried Devices

## Self-acting devices

G switches each device's automatic mode. Only one device of a kind works at a time.

| Device | Effect | Energy | Accessory slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_nutrition_supply" /> | one food point every 0.5 s while hungry | 100 EU a point | belt |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_first_aid_life_support" /> | one health point a second while hurt | 1,000 EU a point | charm |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_force_field_generator" /> | refills the shield below 10 points (Absorption III) | 5,000 EU a refill | necklace |

- With Curios installed, a device works only in its accessory slot. Without Curios it works from the inventory.
- The force field never lowers a larger absorption (a golden apple's, for one).

## Others

- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_fishing_rod" />: 100 EU a reel-in; takes Lure and Luck of the Sea.
- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_lighter" />: right-click places an electric light (500 EU); right-click a light to switch it off; sneak + right-click switches off every electric light within 8 blocks.
