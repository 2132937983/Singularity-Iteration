# Energy Weapons

Right-click to fire. A target hit is safe from damage for about 0.5 s, so it takes at most two hits a second. "Damage a second" below is for one target.

| Weapon | Tier | Damage per shot | Interval | EU per shot | Damage a second |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_electric_rifle" /> | 2 | 6 | 0.1 s | 100 EU | 12 |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_nano_bow" /> | 3 | full draw 15 (about 20 as a critical) | draw about 0.25 s | 500 EU | about 30 |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_advanced_electric_rifle" /> | 4 | 30 | 0.15 s | 3,000 EU | 60 |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_tactical_laser_rifle" /> | 5 | 60 | 0.15 s | 12,000 EU | 120 |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_tachyon_disruptor" /> | 5 | from 120 | 0.25 s | from 50,000 EU | from 240 |

- <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_electric_plasma_gun" /> (tier 3): a slow bolt. 15 on a direct hit; on a block it deals 15 within 3 blocks and makes a small blast. 3,000 EU a shot.
- **Tachyon disruptor.** The bolt pierces targets and blocks and weakens what it hits. Its tuning screen raises output, multi-hit, piercing and true damage; the cost per shot rises steeply.
- **Nano bow.** Uses plain arrows. The arrow flies without gravity and is gone after 10 s in flight. A normal shot takes an arrow, which can be picked up; Infinity or creative takes none.
- **Melee.** With energy, the tactical laser rifle and the tachyon disruptor add +20 / +30 in melee at 1,000 / 1,500 EU a hit, at a sword's attack speed.
- Bolts pass through the shooter's allies. A Wingman's bolts pass through its owner and the owner's other Wingmen.
