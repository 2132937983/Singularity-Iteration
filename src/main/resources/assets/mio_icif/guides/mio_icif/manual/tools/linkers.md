# Link Tools and Power Units

## Frequency transmitter

The <ItemLink id="mio_icif:normal/item_freq" /> pairs two teleporters: right-click the first, then the second. Right-click the air to clear. Both must be in one dimension, and the first one's chunk must be loaded. You need the right to change both.

## Wireless manager

The <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_electric_wireless_manager" /> sets the target of a wireless power node:

1. Sneak + right-click a block that stores EU to record it as the target.
2. Right-click a wireless power node; the node starts sending to the target.

Each action costs 10 EU. Target and node must be in one dimension. Changing a node needs the right to change it.

## Power units

The <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_power_unit" /> and the <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_power_unit_small" /> are crafting parts of electric tools; their charge goes into the result.
