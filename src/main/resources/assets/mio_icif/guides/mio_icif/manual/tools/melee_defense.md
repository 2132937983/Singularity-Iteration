# Melee Weapons and Shield

| Weapon | Tier | Damage | EU per hit | Damage a second |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_nanosaber" /> (lit) | 3 | 21 | 400 EU | about 34 |
| <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_quantum_sword" /> (normal / hyper) | 4 | 25 / 37.5 | 400 / 1,500 EU | 40 / 60 |

- Both swing at a vanilla sword's speed (1.6 a second).
- **Nanosaber.** G lights it (the lighting fee is paid); unlit it deals 5. Lit, it cuts cobwebs fast. With "protect allies" on, it hits tamed animals, villagers and golems as if unlit.
- **Quantum sword.** G switches hyper mode. With too little energy for a hit it deals 5.
- Both take Sharpness, Looting and the other sword enchantments.

## Steel shield

Raising the <ItemLink id="mio_icif:item_tool_steel_shield" /> needs 100 EU. Each point of damage it stops costs 50 EU. When it cannot pay, the hit goes through and the shield cannot be raised for 5 s.
